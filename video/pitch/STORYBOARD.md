# Pitch video — Google Flow + Gemini voice

Ten 8-second clips generated in [Google Flow](https://labs.google/flow), one per pitch-deck beat — pictures and
on-screen English titles both come from Flow. Vietnamese narration is generated by Gemini TTS
(`npm run voice:pitch`) and English subtitles by `node make-srt.mjs pitch/scenes.js` (text in `scenes.js`).
Total ≈ 80 seconds.

## Workflow

1. **Voice first.** `npm run voice:pitch` writes `pitch/audio/<id>.wav` and `manifest.json` with each clip's
   length. Every line must stay under ~7.5 s so it fits its 8 s clip; shorten the text in `scenes.js` and
   rerun `npm run voice:pitch -- <id>` for any line that runs long.
2. **Pictures in Flow.** New project → for each scene paste the *style block* followed by the scene prompt.
   Generate 2–4 variants, keep the best, add it to the Scene builder in order.
3. **Assemble.** Flow cannot import outside audio or subtitle files, so one last step happens in CapCut (or
   Clipchamp): put the 10 clips back to back (each exactly 8 s), place each WAV at the start of its clip,
   lower Flow's own audio to ~10% (ambient only), import `subtitles.en.srt` (CapCut: Text → Local captions →
   Import), export 1080p MP4. The subtitle timings assume exactly that layout.

## Rules that keep Flow output usable

- Write prompts in **English**; Veo follows them more reliably. Narration stays Vietnamese.
- **On-screen titles in English only**, written in the prompt in double quotes, at most ~5 words per title,
  one title per clip. Vietnamese diacritics are still the most likely thing to come out wrong. If a title is
  misspelled, regenerate that clip rather than fixing it later.
- Keep the **bottom fifth of the frame free of text** (prompt: "title at the top"), where the subtitles go.
- **No dialogue.** Every prompt ends with "no speech" so Veo does not invent voices over the Gemini narration.
- **Consistency:** reuse the same style block, and for recurring things (the classroom, the phone app, the
  lecturer) create a reference image once with *Ingredients to video* and attach it to later scenes.
- Use **Extend** when the next beat continues the same shot, **Jump to** for a new shot that keeps context.

## Style block (prefix of every prompt)

```text
Cinematic, modern Vietnamese university campus, clean tech-startup look, soft natural light, cool blue and
white palette with warm accents, shallow depth of field, smooth slow camera motion, 16:9, photorealistic.
```

`Clean bold white sans-serif title text "<title>" fades in at the top of the frame, spelled exactly.`
